Rename the nav-group and plaza modules, and open settings as a route

The sidebar's group-collapse keys and the plaza catalog are named for
what they were when they were written; both now say what they hold.

Settings became a route rather than a modal, so a page inside it has an
address, the back button walks it, and the categories can live in the
sidebar where the rest of the navigation is instead of competing with it
in the content area. The mobile drawer picked up the same treatment.

Labels follow the same instinct: "System settings" carried a word the
page no longer distinguishes itself by, and "Sign out" said more than the
row needs to say.
